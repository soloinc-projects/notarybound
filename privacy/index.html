Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026. This policy covers the Notarybound app for iPhone and Android, made by 8086 LLC, and these web pages.

In short

Notarybound collects no data. There is no account and no sign-in, and there are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server of its own. What the app keeps stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • The state you choose.
  • Your exam date, if you add one. It is only used to count the days.
  • Your practice: the questions you answered and which were right, your accuracy in each area, the questions you missed, your last practice mock's result, and a mock you have not finished.
  • Whether you have unlocked a state.
  • Small settings the app needs, such as when it last asked for a rating.

On iPhone, the app adds up to 15 entries to your phone's own search (Spotlight), one for each state and area, such as “California notary: Misconduct/Fees”, so you can start a drill from search. They hold nothing about you.

None of this leaves your phone. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes all of it, and because there is no account, there is nothing of yours anywhere else to delete.

Buying and restoring an unlock

Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details, no name and no email address. We see the stores' sales reports, which do not identify you. To restore a purchase, the app asks the store which unlocks your store account owns.

Your age range

The first time you open the app, it asks the App Store (Apple's Declared Age Range) or Google Play (Play Age Signals) for your age range, as some U.S. state laws require of apps. The answer is used only while the app is open: it is not stored, not sent anywhere, and changes nothing, because nothing in Notarybound is unsuitable for any age.

Ratings and crash reports

After you finish a practice mock or a drill, the app may ask the App Store or Google Play to show its rating prompt, at most once in 90 days. What you choose there goes to the store, not to us.

Notarybound adds no crash-reporting or analytics tool. If you have chosen in your phone's settings to share analytics or diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may pass us the crash reports and statistics they collect, without your name. That is their feature, under their privacy policy.

These web pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure, under GitHub's privacy statement. We add no cookies, analytics or tracking to these pages.

Email

If you write to support@8086.llc, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use them only to answer you.

Children

Notarybound is for adults applying for a notary commission; California requires applicants to be at least 18. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page changes first, with a new effective date. Questions: 8086 LLC, support@8086.llc.
