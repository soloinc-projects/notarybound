Support

Write to support@8086.llc. Tell us your phone (iPhone or Android) and, for a question you think is wrong, the section on its law card.

Common questions

Which exam does Notarybound cover?

The California notary public exam: practice questions in its five areas, drills by area, and a timed practice mock with the exam's shape, 45 questions in 60 minutes.

Does it replace the course California requires?

No. California requires a six-hour course approved by the Secretary of State before appointment, and a three-hour refresher before reappointment (Cal. Gov. Code §8201). Notarybound is a practice drill and does not satisfy that requirement.

Is this a government app?

No. Notarybound is an independent study aid made by 8086 LLC. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the State of California, the Secretary of State, CPS HR Consulting or any government agency. Its sources are listed on the home page.

Does a practice score mean I would pass?

No, and the app never says so. California passes a scaled score of 70, and a scaled score is not a percent: it cannot be worked out from the number right. So a practice result is practice, not a pass or fail.

I think an answer is wrong.

Open the question's law card. It shows the quoted words and the section the answer rests on. If they do not support the answer, write to support@8086.llc with the section number, and we check the question against its section.

How do I restore my unlock on a new phone?

Sign in to the App Store or Google Play with the same account you bought it with, open Notarybound, and tap Restore purchase on Today or in About.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Practice, the mock and Review all work in Airplane Mode. Buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection, because it goes through the store.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: request a refund from Apple, or from Google Play.

What does the app keep about me, and how do I delete it?

Your state, exam date and practice stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Delete the app to delete them; there is no account, so there is nothing else to delete. See the privacy policy.

Is it accessible?

Notarybound is designed to WCAG 2.2 AA: the largest text sizes, dark mode, VoiceOver and TalkBack. If something gets in your way, tell us at support@8086.llc.
