Study material, not advice

Notarybound is an offline practice drill for the California notary exam. It is not affiliated with any state, is not an approved education course, and is not legal advice. It restates and quotes the law with its section; it does not apply the law to any document or tell you whether a document can be notarized. Notarybound is a practice drill and does not satisfy the course California requires before appointment or reappointment.
